## 2. What Data Sunny Collects

Sunny only collects data necessary to provide its features on the DSB Discord server. This includes:

### 2.1 Discord Data
- **User ID, username, and discriminator/tag** — to identify who issued a command or is being referenced by a feature
- **Server (guild) ID and channel IDs** — to know where Sunny is operating and where to send messages
- **Roles and permissions** — to determine what commands you're allowed to use
- **Message content relevant to commands** — e.g., the text of a command you send to Sunny (Sunny does not read or store messages that are not directed at it, beyond what Discord's API provides for command processing)

### 2.2 Tournament Data (Challonge Integration)
- Tournament names, participant names/IDs, match results, and bracket data submitted or generated through Sunny's Challonge-integration commands

### 2.3 Notification Preferences (Twitch & YouTube Integration)
- **Twitch:** the Twitch channel ID(s) configured for stream-notification features (not your personal Twitch account credentials — Sunny never asks for or stores Twitch passwords or OAuth tokens tied to your personal account)
- **YouTube:** the YouTube channel ID configured for upload-notification features

### 2.4 Technical/Operational Data
- Command usage logs (e.g., timestamps, which command was run) for debugging and abuse prevention
- Data stored in Sunny's local database to support persistent features (e.g., stored tournament records, configured notification channels)

**Sunny does not collect:** your Discord password, payment information, precise location data, or any data from Discord channels/servers it has not been added to.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We process the data above only to:

- Operate Sunny's commands and respond to you
- Run tournament features via Challonge
- Send stream/upload notifications via Twitch and YouTube
- Enforce server rules and prevent abuse of the Bot
- Maintain and debug the Service

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data under the following legal bases:

- **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR** — processing necessary to provide the Service you interact with (i.e., performing the "contract" formed by using a bot's commands)
- **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** — our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving the Bot, and preventing abuse
- **Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR** — consent, where you voluntarily configure optional features (e.g., linking a Twitch or YouTube channel for notifications)

## 6. Your Rights

If the GDPR or a similar data-protection law applies to you, you have the right to:

- **Access** the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **Rectify** inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legitimate operational needs (Art. 17 GDPR)
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing (Art. 18, 21 GDPR)
- **Data portability** for data you provided directly (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for features based on consent (e.g., unlinking a Twitch/YouTube notification)
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Command/technical logs** are retained only as long as needed for debugging and abuse prevention, and are periodically purged.
- **Tournament data** is retained for as long as relevant to ongoing or historical DSB tournaments, or until deletion is requested.
- **Notification configuration data** (linked Twitch/YouTube channel IDs) is retained until the feature is disabled or the server removes Sunny.
- If Sunny is removed from a server, server-specific configuration data is deleted within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legitimate purposes (e.g., abuse records).

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data Sunny processes, including restricting access to the Bot's hosting environment and credentials. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or stored by the Bot.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Sunny is not directed at children under the minimum age required to use Discord under [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms). We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet Discord's minimum age requirements.
